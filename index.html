Meet the founder

Nisha Modi

Nisha started Kynd Bites in her Mumbai kitchen after one too many evenings reading snack labels she could not pronounce. She wanted something she could hand to her own family without a second thought: a snack that tasted like a treat but was built from nuts, seeds, dates and whole oats.

So she went back to the basics her grandmother used. Roasted almonds and pistachios. Pumpkin and sunflower seeds. Rose petals. Honey and jaggery instead of refined sugar. Everything roasted in small batches, mixed by hand and packed fresh every week.

What began as a few boxes for friends became a brand with a simple promise: Kyndness in every bite. Kind to your body, with fibre and protein that keep you full. Kind to your day, because mindful eating should still be delicious. And kind to the people who make it, from the farmers who grow the nuts to the small team that packs every jar.

Good questions

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. Every product is vegetarian. Most are vegan too; granola uses honey, so ask if you need a honey-free batch.

How long do the snacks stay fresh?

Brittles and granola stay crunchy for 6 to 8 weeks in a sealed jar or pouch, away from heat. Bites are best within 4 weeks.

Do you do bulk and corporate orders?

Yes. Diwali hampers, wedding favours and office gifting are a big part of what we do. Message us with quantities and a date.

Can I customise a gift box?

Absolutely. Pick the flavours, add a note, and we will pack it. Lead time is 3 to 5 days for custom boxes.

Where are you based?

Mumbai. Local delivery across the city, and we ship everywhere in India by courier.
